## 48. Debris decay

A long fight leaves the system full of loose junk, and the game simulates every live piece on every machine. Late-game lag and salvage chores are frequent complaints in reviews. So old loose junk with no value glows and then burns up. Code: `src/HotCargo/Debris/`. Numbers: the `Debris` tunables section. Requirements 1200 to 1219.

1200. **Decay.** A junk piece (a ship with junk allegiance, `Ship.IsJunk`) that can decay (requirement 1201) waits, glows (requirement 1203) and then burns up: the game removes it. `Debris.Enabled` (default on) turns the decay off; then junk stays, as in vanilla, and nothing is tracked.
1201. **What stays.** A piece never decays when it is not junk, is an asteroid, has more than `MaxParts` (20) parts (a wreck worth salvage), holds any resource (stored, carried by crew or in transit: tech, mission items, cargo), has crew aboard, is a station or landmark (a special ship component), is the relic (`hc_relic_ship`) or a vanilla abandoned ship (label `abandoned`), is named by a mission (`Mission.IsShipAssociated`), has a part that crew salvage or that lost health to salvage, is in reach of a player's tractor beam that pulls something (the beam's stretch range plus `TractorMargin`, 100), or is locked against removal (`AllowCullShip`). Tech crates and mission item crates are not ships, so they never decay. Limit: a piece is safe only while a beam pulls, not before; the glow warns first.
1202. **Age.** A piece starts to age at the first check at which it can decay. It burns up `DecaySeconds` (240) game seconds later. A piece that becomes protected, goes into stasis or leaves with a jump starts from zero when it can decay again. Limit: junk in stasis never decays, but the game does not simulate it either.
1203. **Glow.** In its last `GlowSeconds` (60) a piece shows a pulsing orange outline on every part. The outline gets brighter and pulses faster toward the burn-up. The glow is drawn from the run state and never changes the sim. Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/debris-glow.jpg` (`tests/screens/debris.txt`).
1204. **Check.** The check runs in the sim tick, every `CheckSeconds` (2) game seconds (a whole number of ticks, at least 1). It walks the live ships in sim order.
1205. **Co-op.** The decay reads sim state only (run game seconds, sim tick, ships, missions, jobs, beams) and changes it only in the sim tick. The ages are run state (`RunState.Debris`), so a save, a load and a co-op resync keep them, and the co-op state hash compares them. The removal skips the game's lost-ship saver, because that reads the local player and writes a local file. So every machine removes the same pieces at the same tick. The co-op steps `debris.decay` and `debris.resync` (`tests/autotest-mp/coop-core.txt`) check this across a resync.
1206. **Tests.** `debris-decay` (single player): small pieces glow and burn up, a big piece and tech crates stay, and the switch off keeps new junk. Unit tests: `DebrisModelTests`.
