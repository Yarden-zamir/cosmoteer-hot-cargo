## 37. Diplomacy

Hails and deals with other ships: hunter demands, surrenders, friendly ships that hold tech or fight for pay, mercenaries. Code: `src/HotCargo/Diplomacy`, the comms hook `src/HotCargo/Ui/CommsHail.cs`, the slider `src/HotCargo/Ui/SliderRow.cs`. Rules: `mod/diplomacy`. Numbers: the `Diplomacy` tunables section. Pure math: `Diplomacy/DiplomacyModel.cs`.

560. **Comms hook.** One Hot Cargo hook adds options to ship comms (`CommsHail`). Areas register a provider. A ship with options from any provider answers calls and shows a dialogue tab. The page starts with the vanilla dialogue (surrender, bounty), then each provider adds text, option rows (requirement 591) and buttons. The check "does this ship answer" runs in the sim and reads sim state only. A ship answers only while it has a real option for the players (a hunter always does; a friendly ship while it may hold tech or help, holds tech, waits for a fee answer or is hired); otherwise it keeps the vanilla behavior. Stations get no diplomacy options.
561. **Demand hail.** The first time a corporate hunter can talk with a human ship (vanilla comms range and sight), it rolls `DemandHailChance` once. On success, and when no other hunter hailed in the last `DemandHailGapSeconds`, it hails the players like a vanilla AI hail. A player can also hail any hunter. Either way the hunter shows the demand page (requirement 562).
562. **Demand page.** The demand page of a hunter has three choices, each with its cost and its result on success and on failure in one line: pay them off (requirements 594 to 596), bluff (requirement 565) and fight ("Come and take it": no cost, the hunter attacks). The general enemy talk stays on the page (requirements 567 and 572). When the hunters leave, the wave leaves: every corporate hunter in the system stops fighting (a truce), loses the hunter tag and its held fuel, and jumps out after `HunterLeaveSeconds`; hunter groups that wait to arrive (requirement 191) do not come; heat drops (requirement 593). A failed pay-off or bluff, and a fight, end that hunter's demand page (requirement 597). Decision (user): one page with three clear choices replaces the hand-over, deal and punishment pages.
563. **Concentration and leverage.** Concentration (for the bluff) = the units of the biggest pile left after the bluff ÷ the units before it. Vaults within `PileDistance` of each other (part centers, a chain counts) form one pile. Leverage (for surrenders) = log4(player strength ÷ their strength), clamped to −1..1. Strength = firepower × health (requirement 47) of the ships within `AreaRadius` of the hailed ship: human ships and ships hired by the players against the hailed ship's team.
564. **Removed.** (Hand over all.) The pay-off tech row stops at all but one unit, so no choice on the demand page ends the run.
565. **Bluff.** "That's all we have": the players give N units and claim it is all. N = `BluffShare` × the vault units, rounded, at least 1 and less than all; no bluff with fewer than 2 units. Chance = `BluffChanceBase` + `BluffPerShare` × N ÷ units − `BluffVisiblePenalty` × concentration. The units go either way. On success the wave leaves as in requirement 562 and heat drops (requirement 593): they believe the tech is gone. On failure they keep the units, heat rises by `BluffCaughtHeat`, and the hunter fights.
566. **Removed.** (Deal.) Money is a row of the pay-off (requirement 594), and the other-hunter rule applies to the whole pay-off.
567. **Demand surrender.** The players can ask any enemy ship with a dialogue module to surrender. Chance = `SurrenderChanceBase` + `SurrenderPerLeverage` × leverage + `SurrenderPerDamage` × lost health + `SurrenderPerFame` × fame title share + `SurrenderPerReputation` × reputation − `SurrenderPerAlly` × allies in the area − `SurrenderWantedPenalty` when its faction wants the players − `HunterSurrenderPerHeat` × heat ÷ 100 for hunters. Reputation = faction reputation ÷ `ReputationScale`, clamped to −1..1.
568. **Once per ship.** Each kind of offer works once per ship: a refused offer stays refused. The run state saves the used offers. Exceptions: the return of held tech, extending a mercenary contract and the answer to a fee question.
569. **Surrender loot.** A ship that surrenders through a diplomacy option gets the vanilla AI surrender (truce, protection, short invincibility, the exit module for ships without a faction) and pays the vanilla payout (ship value × the AI's `AISurrenderPayoutMultiple`) × `SurrenderPayoutScale` × (1 + `SurrenderPayoutPerLeverage` × positive leverage), split between the human players. A corporate hunter pays `HunterSurrenderMoneyShare` (default 0) of it: it gives only its fuel and the chance at cargo. It also gives all its FTL fuel, held hunter fuel (requirement 192) included. A surrendered hunter jumps out after `HunterLeaveSeconds`.
570. **Vanilla surrenders.** A ship that surrenders through the vanilla dialogue (its own offer) gives its fuel and the extra resource of requirement 571 too.
571. **Loot rules.** Fuel goes into the receiving ship, then the chooser's other ships, then the rest of the fleet. Fuel that does not fit drops into space next to the receiving ship as loose nuggets, without a drop roll. With chance `ExtraLootChance` the ship also gives `ExtraLootShare` of one other resource that it carries (not fuel, not tech), only as much as fits.
572. **Hunter yield.** A hunter below `HunterYieldHealth` rolls `HunterYieldChance` once. On success it offers its surrender: it hails a human ship in comms range, and its page shows "accept their surrender" with the payout (none for a hunter by default, requirement 569). Accepting works like requirement 569.
573. **Players' surrender.** The players can offer their own surrender to an enemy ship that is not a hunter. Chance = `OfferChanceBase` − `OfferPerLeverage` × leverage + `OfferPerReputation` × reputation − `OfferWantedPenalty` when wanted by its faction. Price = `OfferPriceUnits` event money units (requirement 73), × `OfferWantedPriceFactor` when wanted. On success the chooser pays and the vanilla player surrender applies: a faction stops wanting the players, a ship without a faction and its team make a truce. Hunters take no surrender offer: their demand page is the players' surrender.
574. **Hold tech.** The players can ask a friendly ship (not a station, not a hunter) to hold N vault units (slider in the row). Military ships of the corporation's ally faction (requirement 640) never hold tech: they work with the corporation. Chance = `HoldChanceBase` + `HoldPerReputation` × reputation − `HoldPerHeat` × heat ÷ 100 − `HoldHuntersPenalty` while any hunter is in the system − `HoldPerUnit` × N, and 0 while a hunter is within `HoldHunterRadius` of the ship. On success the ship may ask a fee first: chance `FeeChanceBase` + `FeePerHeat` × heat ÷ 100 + `FeePerReputation` × reputation, fee = N × tech value × `FeePerUnit`. The players pay or decline.
575. **Held tech counts.** The run state records the holder, its system, the owner, the units and a betrayal roll from the hand-over. Held tech counts in the fleet tech count, so it keeps the run alive (loss rule), adds to the heat multiplier and counts in the score.
576. **Return and betrayal.** Asking for the tech back compares the saved roll with the betrayal chance at that time: `BetrayChanceBase` + `BetrayPerHeat` × heat ÷ 100 + `BetrayPerHunter` × hunters in the system + `BetrayPerReputation` × reputation + `BetrayFeePaid` when the players paid a fee. A betrayal loses the units. Otherwise the units go back into the owner's vaults; units without vault room stay with the ship.
577. **Where held tech is.** A holder in the current system that becomes a wreck spills the units as loose tech nuggets. A holder that is gone (it left the system, or nothing is left of it) takes them. A holder in another system keeps the tech until the players come back. A chat line tells each loss.
578. **Tells.** A holder's ship marker shows "HOLDS n TECH", a hired ship "HIRED m:ss" or "QUITTING", a mercenary "MERCENARY". Results show at the top of the ship's dialogue and in the chat of every player.
579. **Hire help.** The players can offer money (slider, 0 to 2 fair prices in tenths; fair price = `HireFairUnits` event money units) to an armed friendly ship whose AI has the hired module (requirement 580). Chance = `HireChanceBase` + `HirePerOffer` × min(1.5, offer ÷ fair price) + `HirePerReputation` × reputation − `HirePerHeat` × heat ÷ 100, and 0 while the players are wanted. On success the chooser pays and the ship helps for `HireSeconds` (about one mission).
580. **Hired AI.** The `HcHired` strategy module sits at the end of the strategy lists of the Normal, Easy, Wanderer, StationGuard, Police and TradeShip AIs, so it comes before the vanilla modules. It does nothing for ships that nobody hired.
581. **Hired behavior.** A hired ship attacks the enemy of its employer nearest to the employer's nearest ship within `HelpRadius`, if the ship can fight it. With no target it stays within `FollowDistance` of that ship.
582. **Hired ships run.** Below `HiredFleeHealth` (mercenaries: `MercFleeHealth`, lower) a hired ship gives up: it runs from the nearest enemy until `HiredFleeDistance` away, then the contract ends. A contract also ends at its time, when the ship is gone, and when the players jump to another system.
583. **Mercenaries.** An armed friendly ship within `MercStationRadius` of a station rolls `MercChance` once. A mercenary rolls `MercHailChance` once when it can first talk with a human ship, and hails on success. Its page offers a contract by the minute (slider 1 to `MercMaxMinutes`, price `MercRateUnits` event money units per minute). A mercenary always takes a paid contract, also from wanted players (interpretation: mercenaries do not care who pays), and the same option extends a running contract.
584. **Checks.** The sim checks hails, mercenaries, held tech and hires every `CheckSeconds` of game time. The run state saves the next check time, so every machine checks at the same ticks.
585. **Rolls.** Hail and mercenary rolls use a FastRand seeded from the ship ID and the kind of roll. Choice rolls use a seed from the ship, the offer, the sim tick and the result counter.
586. **Save.** The run state saves the used offers and rolls, the mercenaries, the held tech, the hires, the leaving hunters, the fee questions, the last result of each ship and the result counter. The multiselect drafts are in the run state too (requirement 592).
587. **Co-op.** Every choice goes through the shared choice input (domain `diplomacy`, requirement 600 and later), so co-op players see hovers and can vote. The page serial is the ship ID; an option packs the offer and the slider amount (the bluff packs its unit count). The pay-off rows change a shared draft with the ship ID as its serial (requirement 592). The sim checks the choice again when it applies (comms range, amount, money, once per ship) and rejects it otherwise. The caller is the chooser's nearest ship in comms range. Money costs come from the chooser.
588. **Dialog slider.** `SliderRow` is a reusable row: label, slider, value text. An option row (requirement 591) holds one. There is no slider block above the options. The value of an action row is local UI state until its click sends it; the value of a multiselect row is part of the shared draft. The friendly page shows all its rows at once (hold, help or mercenary time, return).
589. **Tests.** Autotests `diplomacy-hunters` (also the pay-off combinations, its success and failure, the bluff, the other-hunter rule, the wave leaving and the heat drop), `diplomacy-surrender` and `diplomacy-friends`, the co-op steps `diplomacy.*` in `tests/autotest-mp/coop-combat.txt` (also a pay-off draft that both players change and a client's submit), and unit tests in `DiplomacyModelTests` and `MultiChoiceModelTests`.

590. **Multiselect pages.** A page can let the players select several options at once, each with its own value (`src/HotCargo/MultiChoice`). An area registers an `IMultiChoiceDomain` at boot (`MultiChoices.Register`), like a shared choice domain. The domain lists the options of an open page (ID, value range and step; no slider when the range is one value) from sim state, or none when the page is closed. The page's Submit and other buttons are shared choices; their Apply reads the draft (`MultiChoices.Draft`).
591. **Option rows.** `Ui/OptionRows.cs` is a reusable list of option rows: a button, and the option's own slider in the same row. A multiselect row shows [X] or [ ] and its slider works only while the option is selected. An action row is a shared choice button with its slider. The comms dialogue tab shows a page's rows above its buttons (`HailPage.AddRow`); every diplomacy slider is in its option's row. The event window shows event options the same way (requirement 810).
592. **Shared draft.** A selection is a shared draft: each select, clear or value change is an MP input (`HotCargoMultiChoice`), checked against the domain's options when it runs, so every player sees the same draft. A dragged slider sends at most every 0.25 s and then its last value. Submit decides through the shared choice system (first pick or vote). The run state saves the drafts; the sim tick drops the drafts of closed pages. Limit: votes on Submit stay when the draft changes; the draft at the decision counts.
593. **Heat drop.** When the hunters leave after a pay-off or a bluff, heat falls by `HandOverHeatDrop` (default 40): they believe the tech is gone. Stages below the new heat start again as heat rises, and fresh waves come through the normal stage system.
594. **Pay them off.** The demand page shows the pay-off rows of a multiselect offer (requirement 590): tech (slider from 1 to all but one vault unit; no row with fewer than 2 units), money (slider in 20 steps up to `PayMoneyMaxUnits` event money units) and crew to cryo jail (slider from 1 to the crew beyond one per human ship, at most `PayCrewMax`; no row when none can go). Chance = min(`PayChanceCap`, crowd × (`PayTechChance` × tech ÷ vault units + `PayMoneyChance` × money ÷ the most money + `PayCrewChancePer` × crew)). Crowd = 1 − other hunters ÷ (max + 1), and 0 with more other hunters than max. Other hunters = corporate hunters within `AreaRadius` of the hailed hunter. Max = `PayMaxOtherHunters` × the co-op enemy factor (`EnemyScale.Cap`). Each row shows what it adds (crowd included); the pay-off button shows the chance live. Decision: no personal bribe row, because with the crowd factor on the whole offer it is a second money row.
595. **Submit.** The pay-off button needs at least one row, the money from the chooser, enough crew, and less tech than the vaults hold. The offered tech leaves the vaults (the chooser's ships first) either way. On success the chooser pays, the crew leave the ship (crew that carry nothing, the chooser's ships first, each ship keeps one), the wave leaves as in requirement 562, and heat drops (requirement 593).
596. **Failure.** On failure the hunters keep the offered tech and that hunter attacks; the players keep their money and crew. Decision (user, confirmed): the tech is gone. There is no timer.
597. **Hunter talks end.** A pay-off, a bluff or a fight ends that hunter's demand page: it takes no second pay-off, bluff or fight, and its draft closes. Other hunters still show their demand page.
598. **API for other areas.** Events (requirement 810) and missions can use multiselect pages: register an `IMultiChoiceDomain`, open a page with a serial, add `HailPage.AddRow` toggle rows (or `OptionRows` in their own window), send `MultiChoiceInput` on a row click or slider change (`MultiChoice/DraftSender.cs` does both, with the slider throttle), and read `MultiChoices.Draft` in their shared choice Apply.
