## 53. AI upkeep

How enemy AI ships keep fighting over a long fight: how long their calls for help take, and how they get repairs. These rules apply to every hostile AI of requirement 360. Tunables: `Tactics`. Code: `Ai/CallStrategy.cs`, `Tactics/FallBackStrategy.cs`, `Tactics/Resupply.cs`. Pure math: `Tactics/RepairModel.cs`.

### Call interception

1300. **Call time.** A call for help (requirements 196 to 199) takes `CallSeconds` (default 22 s). When the call starts, the game checks every human ship that is an enemy of the caller. If one of them has an operational part of a type in `CallInterceptParts` (default the vanilla sensor array `cosmoteer.sensor_array`) whose radar range reaches the caller, the call takes `CallInterceptSeconds` (default 30 s) instead. The call starts at the same time in both cases. It only takes longer. An unknown part ID in `CallInterceptParts` is an error that shows in the overlay.
1301. **Sensor reach.** The radar range of a part is the vanilla radar radius of one sensor (`SensorManager.RadarRadius`): the ship's bounding radius + the part's `RadarRadius` × the radar factor of the ship's nebulas. The distance is from ship center to ship center. Limit: the check runs only at the call start, so a sensor ship that comes into range later does not slow a running call. Revisit when players ask to intercept a running call.
1302. **Tell.** An intercepted call shows a log line to every player, with the part name and the caller: "Sensor array: distress call from {ship} intercepted early. It takes longer." The ring and the "CALLING HELP n%" label of requirement 197 use the duration of that call.
1303. **Determinism.** The check reads sim state only (ship positions, operational sensors, nebulas) in the AI tick, and walks the human ships in sim order. The call duration is module state: saved with the ship and part of the integrity hash. The autotest `ai-call` checks both durations. The co-op step `ai.personalities` intercepts calls on two machines with no desync.

### Retreat and repair

These rules extend fall back (requirement 47) for every AI that runs it (requirement 360). Vanilla facts they build on: an AI ship repairs only with crew and the construction resources on board or on ships of its own player within 400 (`CareerSimModeManager.GetConstructionMode` gives `CrewResources`; `ResourceManager.GetUsableConstructionResourceTotal`). The combat AIs Normal, Easy, Wanderer, StationGuard and Police have no repair module (`Data/ai`), so fall back and flee are their only repair. Crews of an operating station help to repair ships that are not its enemies (`AITradeModule.WillHelpWithConstruction`, set in `ai_space_station.rules`). The no-build radius near combat (`EnemyCombatNoBuildRadius`) does not apply to AI ships (`CareerSimModeManager.GetConstructionLegality`).

1305. **Repair plan.** When a ship starts to fall back, it measures what it lacks: the vanilla cost of all its repairs (every damaged part and every destroyed part and door, `ShipUpdateInfo.GetTotalPhysicalCost`) minus its usable construction resources, per resource type, and its free crew places (`ShipCrewManager.RemainingCrewCapacity`). It is short of crew when its crew is below `FallBackCrewFraction` (default 0.5) of its crew places. Then it picks the first plan that applies:
     1. **Cover**: it lacks no resource, is not short of crew, and a rock gives cover from the nearest enemy (requirement 1306).
     2. **Donor**: it lacks a resource, and a donor ship holds some of it (requirement 1307).
     3. **Station**: it lacks a resource or is short of crew, and a friendly station is in the system (requirement 1308).
     4. **Away**: the fall back of requirement 47: it flies `FallBackDistance` away from the nearest enemy and repairs with what it has.
1306. **Cover.** The ship flies to the hiding spot behind a rock of requirement 366 (the same rocks, search range and gap), on the far side from the nearest enemy human ship on its team radar. The spot follows the threat. When the ship is within `FallBackArriveDistance` (default 100) of the spot and the rock blocks the line to the threat, it holds there and repairs. A lost or unusable rock makes it pick the plan again. With no enemy on radar, it repairs where it is.
1307. **Donor.** A donor is an AI ship that is not an enemy of the ship, is not a station and not junk, is within `FallBackDonorRange` (default 3,000, edge to edge), and holds at least 1 unit of a resource that the ship lacks. Own faction ships and other friendly ships count the same. Vanilla already lets a ship use the stock of its own player's ships within 400, so a donor matters further away or for another friendly player. The ship picks the nearest donor (ties: lower ID), flies to `FallBackDockGap` (default 150) from its hull, and when it is within the vanilla transfer distance (`Crew.LoseHomeShipWarningDistance`, 400, edge to edge) it takes up to `FallBackDonorShare` (default 0.5) of the donor's stock of each lacking resource, at most what it lacks. The units move at once from the donor's storage to the ship's storage (`ResourceManager.RemoveResources`, `AddResources`), like the unload of requirement 1078. Units that do not fit (many combat ships have no storage bays) drop as loose nuggets `60` beside the ship's hull, on the donor's side (`SimNuggetManager.DropNuggets`). The vanilla repair uses nuggets within 400 of the ship (`ResourceManager.GetUsableConstructionResourceTotal`). No money changes hands. Then it repairs as in the away plan, within the trip time (requirement 1309). It visits one donor per fall back.
1308. **Station.** A friendly station is a station that trades with the ship (`ShipAI.WillTradeWith`, the station list of requirement 1069; stations in stasis count when they are not enemies of the ship). Pirates have none. The ship flies to the nearest one (ties: lower ID) to `FallBackDockGap` from its hull. When the station allows trade with it (`SimModeManager.AllowResourceTrade`, within 400), the station supplies it once per visit, for free (enemy AIs have no money limit):
     - Each resource that the ship's own storage lacks for the repairs goes into its storage, and what does not fit drops as nuggets beside its hull, as in requirement 1307. Only its own storage counts, so ships at one station do not count each other's supply.
     - Crew fill the free crew places (`ShipCrewManager.CreateCrewsAtRandomParts`).
     - All repairs are queued (`ShipUpdateInfo.QueuePhysical`, partial work allowed). This skips the money check of the vanilla repair module: next to a trading station the vanilla cost turns the resources that the station sells into credits (`ShipUpdateInfo.GetTotalPhysicalCost` with the final cost), and an AI player has none, so the vanilla module queues nothing there (`AISelfRepairModule.TryRepairObject`). The lacking amounts use the cost without that step.
     Then the ship holds there and repairs, and the station crews help. A station that is gone or becomes an enemy makes the ship pick the plan again.
1309. **Time and return.** The ship returns at `FallBackRecoverFraction`, as in requirement 47. The time limit is `FallBackMaxSeconds` for cover and away, and `FallBackTripSeconds` (default 180 s) for donor and station, counted from the fall back start. The cooldown after a return is the time limit of the last plan.
1310. **Tells.** Movement only (requirement 368): the ship breaks off, flies behind a rock, next to an ally or to a station, sits still while its crew repair, and comes back repaired. No label, marker or log line shows the plan. `Debug.TacticsLog` names each plan, transfer and supply.
1311. **Determinism and switch.** Plans, donors, stations and rocks are picked in the AI tick from sim state, by distance, ties by ID. The plan, its target ID, its place and the supplied flag are module state: saved with the ship and part of the integrity hash. Crew spawn uses the ship's sim random source. `FallBackResupplyEnabled` (default on) switches requirements 1305 to 1309 off: the ship then always uses the away plan.
1312. **Scavenge (later).** Repairs near shipwrecks and loose resources come after the scavenge behavior of the Scavengers area. The hook: a new plan between donor and station in `RepairModel.PickPlan`, with its target from the shared loose resources helper. Until then, no plan collects loot.
1313. **Tests.** Unit tests: `RepairModelTests` (plan order, lacking amounts, donor share). Autotests: `tactics-fallback-cover`, `tactics-fallback-donor`, `tactics-fallback-station` (a damaged faction ship without resources or crew flies to a station, gets supplied and returns repaired). The co-op step `ai.personalities` runs fall back with supply on two machines with no desync.
