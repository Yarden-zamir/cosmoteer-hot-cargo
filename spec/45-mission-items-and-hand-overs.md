## 45. Mission items and hand-overs

A mission that says you carry something gives you a real item. A mission that says you hand something over, or meet someone, has a real action for it. `docs/mission-fidelity.md` lists the gaps that remain.

1100. Mission items are resources. Normal storage holds them, one unit to a storage slot, and stations do not trade them. A destroyed storage destroys the unit half of the time and drops it as a loose crate otherwise. A killed crew member drops a unit that they carry. Items: Sealed Case (`yarden_zamir.sealed_case`, Courier Job), Flight Recorder (`yarden_zamir.flight_recorder`, The Black Box) and Prototype Core (`yarden_zamir.prototype_core_cargo`, Prototype Retrieval).
1101. A mission with a `HotCargoHandOver` objective that has an item and `GiveOnAccept` (the default) gives one unit when it is accepted, inside the game's accept input. The unit goes to the accepting player's first ship, by ship ID, that has room. When no ship of that player has room, the accept input refuses the mission: the accept button is greyed out with "No free storage for the <item>", and a click shows a message. A mission that leaves the log without its hand-over (failed or abandoned) takes one unit out of the players' ships, when the fleet held it.
1102. `HotCargoHandOver` is an exchange in a contact ship's comms. `Page` names a Hail event with `HailTarget = Contact`. While the objective runs (its turn in a sequence), the contact's comms show that page's line, and no other line. The line stays after each use until the objective ends. An outcome with `HandOver = true` completes the objective. With an item, the page's choices take it as a `Cost`, and the line needs a unit in any of its forms (requirement 1117). `ContactAreaTag` marks the contact's area on the map until a player ship has the contact on radar, like vanilla `GotoShip`. Hand-over lines show also when random events are turned off.
1103. A hand-over fails when the contact is destroyed. With an item, it also fails when the fleet held the item and no unit is left in the players' ships (stored or carried by crew) or loose in the system. A message tells the players that the item is lost. A chain stage that fails this way gives the `lost` result (requirement 166).
1104. Courier Job hand-over page (`courier_handover` in `mod/events/events.rules`): "Hand over the sealed case" completes the job. "Hand over the opened case" makes the contact react (requirement 1118). "Not yet" keeps the case. The case opens in the hold, not on this page (requirement 1115).
1105. Extraction (The Quiet Defector, `ChainExtraction`): after the escorts die, the transport's comms show "Get Okafor out." (`okafor_pickup`). Bringing her aboard needs a free bunk and adds 1 crew. The transport's hold is real cargo (requirement 1120). The Debrief page then keeps her, or hands her to the navy or the corporation (she leaves the crew alive, requirement 1109).
1106. An event need can ask for free crew bunks (`Bunks`): the free crew capacity of the human ships in the system.
1107. An item with `GiveOnAccept = false` must be recovered first: the objective line reads "Recover the <item>" until the fleet holds it. `SourceShipTag` names the ship that holds it; the objective puts exactly one unit into that ship the first time it is live. When that ship is gone before the players hold the item and no unit is loose, the hand-over fails. The Black Box: the recorder is in a junk capsule beside the hunter wreck; salvage it, then hand the recorder to the station's broker (`blackbox_broker`). The Playback page follows.
1108. Prototype Retrieval: when the crew lift the core (requirement 133), the nearest human ship with room gets one Prototype Core. With no room in any ship, the lift waits, and the objective line says so. Then hand the core to the broker in the station's comms (`prototype_broker`): hand it over, or ask for more: 0.5 money units more and heat +10 (requirement 1121).
1109. An outcome can remove crew that leave alive (`CrewLeaves`): they go without the death path, so no death counts and no casualty. The result line says that they left the ship. The Debrief page uses it when Okafor goes to the navy or the corporation.
1110. A hand-over can deliver a ship (`ShipTag`): a choice with `Need { HandOverReady = true }` is greyed out, with the reason, until the ship is within `DeliveryRadius` (default 800, hull to hull) of the contact and no player crew is aboard (crew whose home is the ship, or who stand on it). The hand-over removes the ship: the contact takes it. A surrendered ship of a running hand-over does not leave the system, and its crew fly it to the contact. The hand-over fails when the ship is gone. Repossession uses it: after the capture, the bank at the station takes the freighter (`repo_handover`).
1122. An outcome can drop its gains in space (`DropNear`, a ship tag). Its Tech and resource gains drift loose as crates beside the ship with that tag that is nearest to the fleet: live, a wreck (a wreck keeps its tags) or in stasis. With no such ship in the system, they drift beside the fleet. The result line says that they drift loose. The crew picks them up like any crate.
1123. Chain stages whose text calls the reward cargo use `DropNear`. Courier Run: the courier's crew pushes the tech crate out of the airlock (`hc_ch_cargo`). Okafor's Depot: a tech crate drifts out of the wreck. Take It Back: the 3 processors drift out of Rook's wreck (`hc_ch_enemy`). No stage puts cargo straight into the hold.
1124. Hold the Pass promises no convoy: its text says to keep a ship in the ring until the timer runs out (requirement 222, `PassHoldSeconds`).
1125. Sundive: the salvage puts one Flight Recorder (`Recorder` in the rules) into the storage of the ship that finished it. With no free storage, the salvage waits and the objective line says so. The escape completes when a human ship that holds the recorder is out of every heat zone. The job fails when no recorder is left in the players' ships or loose in the system. When the mission leaves the log, the client takes the recorder (one unit). A hand-over that leaves the log without its hand-over takes its unit back the same way (requirement 1101).
