## 45. Mission items and hand-overs

A mission that says you carry something gives you a real item. A mission that says you hand something over has a real action for it. `docs/mission-fidelity.md` lists the gaps that remain.

1100. Mission items are resources. Sealed Case (`yarden_zamir.sealed_case`, `mod/events/sealed_case.rules`): normal storage holds it, one case to a storage slot, and stations do not trade it. A destroyed storage destroys the case half of the time and drops it as a loose crate otherwise. A killed crew member drops a case that they carry.
1101. A mission with a `HotCargoHandOver` objective gives one unit of that objective's item when it is accepted, inside the game's accept input. The unit goes to the accepting player's first ship, by ship ID, that has room. When no ship of that player has room, the accept input refuses the mission: the accept button is greyed out with "No free storage for the <item>", and a click shows a message. A mission that leaves the log without its hand-over (failed or abandoned) takes one unit out of the players' ships.
1102. `HotCargoHandOver` (`ResourceType`, `ContactShipTag`, optional `ContactAreaTag`): the map marks the contact's area until a player ship has the contact on radar, like vanilla `GotoShip`. While the objective is open and the fleet holds the item, the contact's comms show a Hail line (`HailTarget = Contact`, requirement 769). The line stays after each use until the objective ends. The page's choices take the item as a `Cost`, and an outcome with `HandOver = true` completes the objective.
1103. A hand-over fails when the contact is destroyed, or when no unit of its item is left in the players' ships (stored or carried by crew) or loose in the system. A message tells the players that the item is lost.
1104. Courier Job hand-over page (`courier_handover` in `mod/events/events.rules`): "Hand it over" completes the job. "Open it first" also completes it: 60 % pays 0.5 money units more with heat +8; 40 % + 0.3 × heat is a tracer with heat +15. "Not yet" keeps the case.
