## 10. Corporate response

30. `HeatStages` is a list of stages. Each stage has a threshold and a response. The list decides the stage count, and the last stage is the Strike stage. Warning is above the highest heat that a jump carries in (requirement 650), so it always starts after arrival. Hunt starts with the station markup (requirement 655). Defaults:

| Stage | Heat | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | 40 | Alert sound and message. Scouts: 1 ship of the sector tier in sector 1, 2 in the last sector (requirement 190). |
| Hunt | 75 | Three hunter ships of the sector tier + 1. |
| Strike | 100 | A strike force: 2 ships of the sector tier + 1 in sector 1, up to 3 ships of tier + 2 in the last sector (requirement 190). The ships arrive one at a time, 45 s apart (requirement 191). A new wave every `StrikeWaveIntervalSeconds` (default 180 s) while heat stays at 100. |

31. Each stage starts once for each sector visit. The Strike stage repeats its waves. A stage response can arrive in groups (requirement 191). A group spawns only enough ships to reach `MaxLiveHunters` (default 12) live hunters in the system. In co-op the group and the cap scale (requirement 334).
32. Hunters spawn at a distance of `SpawnDistanceMin` to `SpawnDistanceMax` (default 2,000 to 3,000) from the center of the player fleet. All ships of one wave come from one direction, spread over `WaveArcDegrees` (default 40°). They always know where the players are, move to them and attack. Hunters far from the players move toward them in stasis too.
33. Hunters are a corporate force, not a rules faction. They use built-in ships with the `ShipTags` tag (default `combat`) from the `ShipFactions` pools (default: all factions), at the wanted tier or the nearest lower tier. Their names start with `ShipNamePrefix` (default "Corporate"). All hunters in a system share one hostile player index, so they are allies of each other and enemies of the players. Like vanilla ambushes, they are also enemies of faction ships, except the ally faction's ships in corporate ally space (requirement 1150). Every hunter carries FTL fuel (requirement 192). Hunters give no fame and no money reward, so a fight with them adds no heat; their wreck pays in salvage, about 10 % of the ship value on the Fast economy (BALANCE.md). Hunters never offer or accept surrender through the vanilla dialogue; the diplomacy hails of section 37 handle their demands and surrenders. Resolved: a rules faction needs an icon, two player indices and its own ship pool, and the faction reputation systems would treat it as a normal faction. A unique player index gives the same result at much lower cost.
34. Hunters prefer to attack ships that carry Tech Vaults. A ship with a Tech Vault part counts `VaultTargetFactor` times more (default 3) when a hunter picks the nearest target.
35. The Strike stage is dangerous but survivable. Its ships arrive in groups, so players who can fight have time to destroy a hunter, take its fuel and jump away (requirements 191 and 192). It does not end the run directly. Only a loss under requirement 9 ends the run. **Open decision:** the user may choose a hard fail instead.
