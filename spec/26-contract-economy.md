## 26. Contract economy

A contract is a mission offer that a client gives: every offer in the station comms missions tab and the system and galaxy lists. Code: `src/HotCargo/Contracts`. Numbers: the `Contracts` tunables section.

100. **Terms.** Each contract has terms (`ContractTerms`): accept fee, completion bonus, flat heat on accept, trail weight, rival group and informant chance. Rules terms come first: a mission definition can put `{ Type = HotCargoContract; AcceptFee; FlatHeat; TrailWeight; RivalGroup; InformantChance }` in its Rewards list, and a missing field uses the default. Then `Contracts.Metatypes` gives terms for vanilla mission types. The rest comes from a roll seeded by the mission ID, so every client gets the same terms. The sim tick saves the terms of new offers once per second. Missions of a category in `ExcludedCategories` (default: exploration) are not contracts. Per rule:
     - Hot Cargo missions: Convoy Duty leaves half a trail (0.5) and rolls the rest. Joint Strike has flat heat 5, no fee and no informant. Discreet Bounty has a fee of 0.5, flat heat 4 and no informant (its trap is its own sell-out).
     - Vanilla missions: fee, trail and informant use the roll. Pirate and faction base attacks have flat heat 5.
     - Mechanics missions (requirement 130 and later), chain missions (requirement 160 and later) and event missions (requirement 86) carry their own `HotCargoContract` terms. The same UI, accept input, trail and informant rules apply to them. Missions that go straight to the log (not offered) are not contracts.
101. **Trail.** Open contracts (accepted, in progress, with terms) leave a trail. Heat per game minute = (sum of trail weights − `TrailFreeContracts`) × `TrailHeatPerContractMinute`, never below 0. Defaults: 3 free, 1 heat per minute for each contract above 3. The trail ignores the tech multiplier.
102. **Fees.** About half of the contracts (`FeeChance` 0.5) cost a fee. Model: purchase. The player who accepts pays the fee and does not get it back. A fee is `FeeMin` to `FeeMax` (0.15 to 0.4) standard mission pays at the mission tier, with the difficulty money factor and `RewardScale`, rounded to 500. A paid contract pays a bonus of fee × `PaidBonusFactor` (2) on completion to the player who paid the fee, so fee and bonus stay with one player in co-op. If that player left the game, the bonus is split like vanilla mission money. So a paid contract nets one fee more than a free one, and a failed one loses the fee. The bonus is a reward line on the offer (`HotCargoContractBonus`). A player with less money than the fee cannot accept: the accept button shows the fee and does not react, and the accept input refuses. The difficulty fee factor applies on top (requirement 682).
103. **Rivals.** Offers in one system with the same rival group are rivals, each of all the others, so the relation is symmetric. Accepting one dismisses the others, and a chat line names them. `RivalsEnabled` (default on) turns all rivals off, the rival groups of rules terms too, and at once also for offers that got a rival group before the switch. The scan pairs a new offer with an earlier offer of another client (another faction or another station): always when one offer targets the other's faction, otherwise with chance `RivalPairChance` (0.2). A new offer in the group of an open contract is dismissed.
104. **Informants.** `InformantShare` (75 %) of contracts carry an informant chance of 5 to 15 %. The accept rolls it with the simulation random source. On a hit, `InformantDelayMin` to `InformantDelayMax` (45 to 150) game seconds later, if the contract is still open, the client sells the players out: heat + `InformantHeat` (15) and a hunter wave of `InformantShips` (2) at the sector tier. A banner and a chat line name the client and the contract, and the mission log shows "<client> sold you out on this contract".
105. **Smaller systems.** For shorter in-system travel, gameplay spawns move in to `SystemScale` (0.5) of the vanilla radius: trade and military stations, bounties, wanderers and pirates, asteroid fields and belts, loose asteroids, discoverables, beacons and the jump gates (9,375 to 11,250). Each distance is a formula of the vanilla value and the scale; counts of scattered objects drop with the area (scale squared). Gaps between gameplay objects scale too. Sun gaps never go below the sun heat zone plus 1,500 (5,250 from the sun center), so no station, site or ship parked at a station is in the heat. Trade stations sit 5,250 to 7,000 from a single sun (the sun gap plus the scaled vanilla ring width 3,500), inside the gate ring, and there are 2 to 3 per system (was 2 to 4). Fewer missions: 2 to 4 bounties (was as many as fit), 1 delivery per station (was 2), Convoy Duty 45 % (was 60 %), Joint Strike 30 % (was 40 %), one Discreet Bounty at 60 % (was 1 to 2 at 70 %). Scenery (suns, planets, nebulas, fog) keeps the vanilla size. Military stations keep the vanilla gaps times the scale (3,000 from stations, 1,500 from gates). Hot Cargo sites follow the scale too: mechanics missions (requirement 130 and later), event signals, the courier contact and the relic ship, the archetype objects (requirement 229), chain and contract sites. Short hops next to a station or the fleet (under about 6,500) keep their distance, so fights do not start next to stations. The Buyer (requirement 1) spawns between the sun heat and the gate ring and keeps 3,000 from bounty ships, which share its ring. The scale is in `mod/contracts/system_scale.rules`; the vanilla changes and the mission counts are rules in `mod/contracts/actions.rules` and `mod/missions/sysgen_hotcargo.rules`; the Hot Cargo sites are in the rules of their areas. The autotest `worlds-rings` checks the gate ring, the station ring, the sun heat, the military station fit and the Buyer place in basic, binary and supernova systems. The vanilla changes change the shared sector rules, so they apply to every map size while the mod is on, and need a game restart.
106. **Contract UI.** The mission info panel (comms missions tab and mission log) has a "CONTRACT" section under the rewards. Before the accept it shows the fee (and the player's money when it is too low), the flat heat, the trail ("open contracts X → Y, heat +a → +b/min", or "still quiet"), every rival offer that the accept dismisses (a comms tab shows only its own station list and the system and galaxy lists, so a rival that another station offers reads "at <station>"), and the informant risk or "Discreet client". After the accept it shows the fee paid, the open count and trail rate, the informant risk, and a sell-out. The accept button reads "Accept (pay N)". Mission titles are XML (a chain stage title has a color tag): `Ux/MissionTitle` gives the title as XML for formatted text and as plain text for logs and telemetry. The offer details title and the vanilla new, completed and failed mission banners show the title without raw tags. Accepting and paying go through the game's own accept input (`MPAcceptMissionInput`), so they are deterministic in multiplayer.
107. The run state saves the terms, the pending sell-outs and the sold-out contracts. Accepted contracts with a bonus keep a `HotCargoContractBonus` reward, so like other Hot Cargo missions such a save needs the mod.
108. After a win, the contract economy stops: no fees, no flat heat, no trail and no sell-outs.
