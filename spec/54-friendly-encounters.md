## 54. Friendly encounters

Ships that meet the players as friends and ask for something: to join, to learn, to trade, a riddle, a debt, or help in a fight. Code: `src/HotCargo/Friendly`. Pages: ship-carried events in `mod/events/events.rules` (requirement 767), ships in `mod/events/spawns.rules`. Text: `mod/friendly/strings.en.rules`. Design rule (section 46): a dialog offers clear deals; every outcome shows in the world and in a message.

1360. **Effects.** An event outcome can name a C# effect (`Effect`). An area adds its effects to `EventRunner.Effects` in Register, before the events load. The event rules check at load that each named effect exists, and option effects take none. An effect runs with the other outcome effects in the choice input (sim logic, every machine), after them, and adds its result lines. The Friendly area adds the effects of this section. Choices on these pages are shared choices (requirement 771), so co-op players see hovers and votes.
1361. **Join request.** Event `join_request` (Arrival, weight 5): a small armed ship (`Recruit`, one tier below the sector) carries the page. "Take them in." is a clear deal: it costs 1 money unit (requirement 73), and the effect `join` makes the carrier a ship of the chooser: vanilla allegiance (`ShipMetadata.PlayerIndex`), no AI, no event carrier tag, no stasis handler, and its crew take the same allegiance. The ship shows in the player's colors, and a center message and a chat line say "<ship> joins your fleet." A carrier that is gone before the choice joins nobody, and the result says so. "Not now." does nothing. Tests: autotest `friendly-join`, co-op step `friendly.join` (`tests/autotest-mp/coop-friendly.txt`).
1362. **Apprentice.** Event `apprentice` (Arrival, weight 5): a small armed ship (`Recruit`) asks to follow the players and learn. "Let them follow." is free. The effect `apprentice` gives the ship a Diplomacy hire for the chooser (requirements 580 to 582: it follows, fights the employer's enemies, and runs when hurt) for `Friendly.ApprenticeMinutes` (6) game minutes. A center message and a chat line say how long it follows and how many fights it wants to see (`ApprenticeJoinFights`, 3). "Not now." does nothing.
1363. **What the apprentice saw.** An enemy ship of the players that becomes a wreck (vanilla `OnShipJunked`) within `ApprenticeWatchRadius` (2,500) of an apprentice counts as a fight that it saw. Its ship label shows "APPRENTICE n/m FIGHTS". At its time it decides: with at least `ApprenticeJoinFights` fights it joins the chooser's fleet as in requirement 1361 ("<ship> saw n fights with you. It joins your fleet."); with fewer it thanks the players and jumps out (the Diplomacy leaving jump, "<ship> saw n of m fights. It leaves."). When its hire ends early because it ran from damage, it leaves at once with its own message. It cannot jump with the players: on a system jump it stays behind, and a chat line says so. A ship that is gone ends it without a message. The state (ship, player, time, fights) is in the run state; the count and the decision run in sim logic. Tests: autotest `friendly-apprentice`, co-op step `friendly.apprentice`.
1364. **Wandering trader.** Event `wandering_trader` (Arrival, weight 5): a civilian hauler (`Trader`) carries a page with trade rows (page options, requirement 810): 40 steel for 1 processor, 10 coils for 10 tristeel, 40 hyperium for 1 diamond, each 1 to 3 times. Each trade gives about twice the vanilla station value (`BuyPrice`) of what the players pay. The row says what goes and what comes, so it is a clear deal. "Make the trades." pays the selected rows from the fleet and adds what they give. Then the effect `leave` makes the trader jump out after `Friendly.LeaveDelaySeconds` (5) with the vanilla FTL jump-out effect (the Diplomacy leaving jump), and a chat line names it. "Not today." does nothing. Tests: autotest `friendly-trader`, co-op step `friendly.trader`.
1365. **Riddle ship.** Events `riddle_hole`, `riddle_keys`, `riddle_dark`, `riddle_stamp`, `riddle_egg` (Arrival, weight 1 each, heat 10 or more): a painted civilian ship (`RiddleShip`) asks one riddle with three answers. The pool opens once per run: every answer sets the flag `riddle_met`, which the pool's need excludes. "No time for games." is the safe last choice and sets nothing.
1366. **Riddle answers.** A right answer runs the effect `halve_heat`: heat × `Friendly.RiddleHeatKeep` (0.5) ("they talk to their contacts"). The heat bar drops, and a center message and a chat line name the old and the new heat. A wrong answer adds heat +3, and the result says they told the story on an open channel. Tests: autotest `friendly-riddle`, co-op step `friendly.riddle`.
1367. **Debtor.** Event `debtor` (Arrival, weight 5): a small armed ship (`Recruit`) cannot pay its debt. "Buy their debt." costs 1.5 money units; the effect `debtor` records the price in credits (the choice's cost, as the page showed it) and gives the ship a Diplomacy mercenary hire for the chooser (requirements 580 to 582) for `Friendly.DebtorMinutes` (30) game minutes. A center message and a chat line say the time and the amount it pays back. "Not my problem." does nothing.
1368. **Debt paid or lost.** A debtor's ship label shows "DEBTOR OWES <credits> <m:ss>". At its time the debtor pays the chooser the full price (the same credits), its hire ends, and it jumps out (the Diplomacy leaving jump); a center message and a chat line say so. If it dies first (a wreck, or gone and not in stasis, in its own system), the debt is lost, with a message. When the players jump, it cannot follow: it stays behind, cannot die there, and pays at its time; a chat line says so. A debtor that runs from damage (requirement 582) stops following but keeps its debt. Tests: autotest `friendly-debtor`, co-op step `friendly.debtor`.
1369. **The Wayfarers.** A friendly fleet with its own allegiance (player index 99,000, `PayItForward.Allegiance`), not a vanilla faction, so it stays out of galaxy generation, reputation and trade routes (coordinator decision). It is not an enemy of the players or of faction civilians, and it is an enemy of every allegiance that is an enemy of a human player (a patch on the career `AreEnemies`). Its ships show the label "WAYFARER". Limit: a faction rules entry with that index would clash.
1370. **Help when outclassed.** Every `Friendly.PayForwardCheckSeconds` (2) of game time, while no Wayfarers help, the sim compares strengths (Diplomacy strength: firepower × health): the players' ships against the enemy ships within `PayForwardRadius` (3,000) of a player ship. When the enemies have at least `PayForwardOutclassRatio` (2) times the players' strength for the first time in a system (a qualifying fight), the system rolls once for the run (a FastRand from the system ID): with chance `PayForwardChance` (0.15) the Wayfarers come (`Wayfarers` in spawns.rules: 2 combat ships, 1,200 to 1,600 from the fleet). They come at most once per run: the run state saves that they came (`PayForwardDone`), so a load does not reset it, and no system rolls after that. They take their allegiance (crew too) and a Diplomacy mercenary hire for the first human player, for at most `PayForwardFightMinutes` (10), so they fight the players' enemies (requirements 580 to 582). A center message and a chat line say they came.
1371. **Pay it forward.** When no enemy has been near the players for `PayForwardQuietSeconds` (10), the Wayfarers' hires end, every Wayfarer repairs all its parts to full health (a center message says so), and their leader hails the players with the page `pay_it_forward` ("Pay it forward to the next one in need."). With another event page open, the words go to the center and the chat instead. The answer "We will." (effect `wayfarers_leave`), or `PayForwardLeaveSeconds` (60) without one, makes them jump out (the Diplomacy leaving jump) with a chat line. When all of them die, the help ends. A system jump leaves them behind. Tests: autotest `friendly-forward`, co-op step `friendly.forward`.
