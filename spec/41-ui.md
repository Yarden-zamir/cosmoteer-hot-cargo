## 41. UI: mission select, icons and event options

Faster, clearer choices: the mission lists, kind icons, and multiselect options on event pages. Code: `src/HotCargo/Ux/MissionSelect.cs` (switch `Ux.MissionSelect`), the event options in `src/HotCargo/Events` (`EventRunner`, `EventView`, `EventsMultiDomain`), icons in `mod/ux/actions.rules`. Pure math: `Ux/MissionSelectModel.cs`, `Events/EventModel.cs`.

### Mission select

800. **Mission lists.** The comms missions list and the mission log show each mission's kind icon left of its name (requirement 805). Chain stages show in the chain color, and with "group by mission type" their group comes first, in the same color. The tooltip of a mission names its kind and its risk.
801. **Filter.** Both lists get a filter: all missions, Hot Cargo (chains and shady offers included), story chains, contracts (missions with contract terms, requirement 100), standard (vanilla) missions, and "no shady offers". The filter hides missions the way the vanilla "local system only" box does.
802. **Distance and risk.** The right side of each mission shows the distance in this system, or the jumps for another system ("2 jumps", "no route"), then its risk: the heat it adds on completion (fame heat by requirement 25, an escort's heat change, and the contract's flat heat), a shady offer's trap chance at today's heat (requirement 39), and a contract's informant chance as "leak". The vanilla time left follows.
803. **Sort and memory.** The sort list gets "Heat and risk": lowest risk first. Risk = heat + `RiskHeatPerChance` × (trap chance + informant chance). The vanilla sorts by credits, distance and mission type stay. The sort, grouping and filter choices stay per player for each list kind (comms offers, mission log) for the game session. They are UI state only: never saved, never sent.
804. **Chain offers.** A station with a story chain offer shows a "Story chain" badge in the comms list and in its hover text (UX V11). The first station of a run always gets a chain (requirement 160), so the badge shows there at once.

### Icons

805. **Kind icons.** Each Hot Cargo mission type has an inline icon, the text sprite `hc_mission_<metatype ID>`. The code finds it by that name, so a new mission type gets its icon from the rules alone. Markers get icons too: the Buyer tag, held tech, hired ships and mercenaries, and the behavior labels of corporate hunters. All art is vanilla (roof decal icons and GUI icons), tinted by the text color.

### Win screen

806. The win panel has two buttons: "End run" (primary, Enter) returns to the main menu, like the game-over screen's quit; "Continue" closes the panel. In co-op only the host gets "End run".

### Mission descriptions

807. **Short descriptions.** Every Hot Cargo mission description (Hot Cargo, mechanics, event and world missions, chain stage briefs, the Buyer mission) has at most 2 short lines. Line 1 says what to do. Line 2 names the catch or risk, only if there is one. Pay, heat, distance, faction and system show in the reward list, the risk and distance columns and the info panel, not in the text. A chain's story lines show in the tooltip of its "Story chain, part N" line (requirement 170).

### Event page options

810. **Options.** An event page can have up to 4 options (`Options` in the event rules), shown as multiselect rows (requirement 591) above its choices, in the event window and in a carrier's comms. An option pays with nothing, money, tech, crew who leave, crew on hand (they stay), or a resource. Its slider picks steps of `StepUnits` from `MinUnits` to `MaxUnits`; one unit is `Unit` (+ `UnitPerTier` per tier above 1, + `UnitPerHeat` × heat when the page opened). The selection is a shared draft (requirement 592, domain `events`), so co-op players see one draft.
811. **Submit.** A choice with `NeedPoints` above 0 submits the options. It needs at least one selected option and points ≥ `NeedPoints` + `NeedPointsPerHeat` × heat; each option gives `Points` per unit. The sim checks each selected option's need and all pays on top of the choice's own need and cost (an option's need adds to its pay, so "Need Tech 1" with a crate keeps one crate), then pays them from the chooser and the fleet. A selected `Sure` option takes the first outcome without a roll; otherwise the outcome rolls as before. `ScaleByPoints` scales the outcome's money and fame by the points. The other choices of the page ignore the draft. The window shows a greyed submit with "Select an option first" or "Offer more".
812. **Option effects.** An option's `Effect` applies per unit (money, fame, heat, reputation, tech, crew, resources and hull damage scale; flags apply once), `Once` applies once, and `Risk` applies with chance `RiskChance` × units, rolled from the page seed. Effect texts show as result lines. Option effects take no follow-up page, spawn, chain result, Buyer price or offer.
813. **Balance.** The converted events cost the same for the same result as their old choices: Corporate Checkpoint, The Magpies, The Magpies Return, Pirate Toll Gate, Vault Coolant Leak, A Clean Record, Refugee Convoy, The Fence, Off the Books, Running on Empty and Dead in Space (EVENTS.md). New mixes (steel or crew for a toll, a crate at the checkpoint) are designer numbers in `mod/events/events.rules`.
814. **Keys.** Keys 1 to 9 count the option rows first, then the buttons (UX V15). A digit on a multiselect row toggles it. In single player, Enter picks the first active submit of a page with rows (an event submit, or the pay-off button of a hunter, requirement 594); in co-op Enter opens the chat, so the digit of the submit button does it.
815. **Tests.** Autotests `ux-missions` (icons, chain badge, filter and its memory), `ux-more` (a digit toggles a row, Enter submits), `events-options` (a sale by amount, mixed parts on a carried page, refugees), the updated `events-*` tests, the co-op tests `events-options` (a client's draft, the host's submit) and `wanted` (a client's crate option), the updated co-op tests `events`, `hail` and `sharedchoice`, and the unit tests `MissionSelectModelTests` and `EventModelOptionTests`.

Not converted: Listening Post. Its intensive scans are mission panel orders (one click each, at most `MaxIntensivePerWave` per wave), not an event page, so a multiselect page does not apply.
