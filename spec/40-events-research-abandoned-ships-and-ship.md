## 40. Events: research, abandoned ships and ship comms

More of the Events area (section 22, EVENTS.md).

### Research and abandoned ships

760. Research trade: an event page can carry a research trade, built from the vanilla tech tree (`CareerModeRules.Techs`) when the page opens. Candidates: techs not researched, prerequisites met. Research value of a tech = its price at a Tech Provider here: `Cost` × the players' `TechBuyCostFactor` with the faction that controls the system (1 without one) × the station markup for heat (requirement 655). The trade asks for `ceil(value of the first tech ÷ (tech value × ResearchRate))` tech units, at most `ResearchMaxUnits`, and adds more techs (at most `ResearchMaxTechs`) while the total stays within units × tech value × `ResearchRate`. It is valid only when the total research value is at least units × tech value × `ResearchMinRate` (above 1), so it always beats buying. The order of candidates is shuffled with the page seed; when that order yields no trade, the ID order is used. An event with an offer opens only when an offer can be built.
761. Research events: The Archive (Station trigger: trade or keep) and A Lab That Won't Take No (Arrival, extortion: trade, pay 0.6 money units, or refuse for heat +20). `ResearchWeightScale` multiplies the trigger weight of research events.
762. Research sale (Data Broker, Arrival): the buyer pays `ResearchSalePremium` × the research value of researched techs for copies, at most `ResearchSaleCap` money units; the players keep the research. A heat-weighted outcome adds heat +15 (a tracker in the transfer).
763. The page's offer shows through text tokens: `{offer_techs}`, `{offer_units}`, `{offer_worth}` (the tech value of those units), `{offer_value}` (the research value), `{offer_price}`. A choice cost `OfferUnits` pays the trade's units (the chooser's fleet vaults). Outcome effects: `UnlockOffer` unlocks the techs (TechManager.UnlockTech), `OfferPrice` pays the sale price (split like a mission reward).
764. Abandoned ships: when a human ship comes within `AbandonedTriggerDistance` of a vanilla abandoned ship (label `abandoned`), it rolls `AbandonedEventChance` once per ship (the Abandoned trigger, no cooldown or system cap). Events: Abandoned Ship (board: loot, fuel, nothing, or a corporate trap that grows with heat) and Cold Hulk (drain the tanks: fuel, sometimes hull damage).
765. Relic ship: each run picks one sector from `RelicMinSector` to the sector before the Buyer (`RelicEnabled`). On the first arrival there, a dead ship with 1 tech unit spawns 5,000 to 8,000 away (junk allegiance, label `abandoned`, tag `hc_relic_ship`). Within `RelicMessageDistance` the vault senses it once (top message and chat line). Within `RelicBoardDistance` the Relic event opens; leaving and coming back opens it again until the crate is taken (flag `relic_taken`). Recovering it needs 1 vault slot; a heat-weighted outcome also spawns a corporate patrol.
766. All research and abandoned-ship choices go through the choice input (requirement 74), costs come from the chooser, and their hostile spawns are rules spawners (scaled by requirement 332).

### Events in ship comms

767. Ship-carried events: an event can name a `Carrier` generator in spawns.rules. When the page opens, the carrier ship spawns (tag `hc_ev_carrier`), and the page shows in that ship's comms dialogue tab instead of the event window: the page text, then one button for each visible choice (greyed out with the reason as in requirement 68). With `CarrierHails` (default on), a chat line says that the ship is calling, and the ship hails the players (vanilla `AIHailPlayer`) once a player ship is in comms range. The players can also hail it. The carrier stays a normal ship: players can attack it, leave it, or tow it. First case: Dead in Space (its freighter; the tow choice tows that same ship).
768. A Station event is carried by the station that triggered it: the station hails the players and its comms show the page. A follow-up page (`Next`) stays on the ship that carried the page before it.
769. Hail lines: an event with `Trigger = Hail`, a `HailLine` and a `HailTarget` (`Station`: trade stations, not the Buyer; `FactionShip`: faction ships that are not stations and not hostile) adds a button with its line to the comms of matching ships while no page waits and its need is met. A click sends the `HotCargoEventHail` MP input, which opens the page on that ship. Each ship offers each line once per run. Lines: "Sell a crate off the books." (stations) and "Ask about hunters nearby." (faction ships, heat 10 or more). The contact of a running hand-over objective (`HailTarget = Contact`) shows only its own page's line, with no once-per-ship limit (requirement 1102).
770. A carried page closes when its ship is gone. Destroyed: the page ends with the result "The <ship> was destroyed, and its offer with it." (without the name when the ship is no longer in the sim). Left in another system: the page closes with no result page, because the players chose to leave. A station that refuses calls (requirement 657) never carries a Station event, and a page on a station that starts to refuse closes with "The station stops answering." A choice input for that page does nothing (serial, requirement 74).
771. The page's choices in comms are shared choices (`HailPage.AddChoice`, section 32): co-op players see hovers and votes, and a pick sends the same choice input (`HotCargoSharedChoice`, requirement 74) with the page serial, so two players in the comms of the same ship cannot apply a page twice. Ship comms go through the one Hot Cargo comms hook (`Ui/CommsHail`, an `IHailProvider` for each area): the Events area registers one provider, and the diplomacy area adds its hails (hunter demands, surrender, friendly ships) through its own provider. Events registers first: on a ship that carries an event page, the event owns the page's shared choices and diplomacy's choices do not show (one shared domain per page); Hot Cargo hail lines are plain buttons and show next to diplomacy's choices. A hail line opens its page in the comms tab that is already open, so the tab wires shared choices to the page that it shows now, not only to its first page. A shared choice that the tab did not wire reports an error, never does nothing (autotest `events-offbooks` clicks the real comms buttons).
