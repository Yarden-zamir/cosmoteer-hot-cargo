## 46. World actions

Design rule (section 2): risky actions and gambles happen in the world. A dialog offers only clear deals. This section covers item actions in storage, the Sealed Case, the patrol listen, hulk loot and the gambles that moved out of dialogs.

1111. An item's resource rules list its context actions in `HotCargoActions`: `ID`, `NameKey`, `ToolTipKey`, `Becomes`, `Contents`, `MessageKey`. When a player selects a storage part that holds a unit of such an item, on a ship that the player commands, the parts card (lower left) shows one button per action. The buttons sit with the vanilla part trigger and toggle buttons. A button shows the item's icon; its tooltip shows the action name and text.
1112. An action takes one unit out of the selected part. `Becomes` puts its item into the freed slot. One of `Contents` (weights `Weight` + `WeightPerHeat` × heat) goes into the same part: the freed slot first, else a slot that takes it, else it drifts loose beside the ship. An action without `Becomes` and `Contents` uses the unit up. The roll uses the sim random generator.
1113. A press sends the MP input `HotCargoItemAction` (part, item, action), so it runs on every machine in co-op. It does nothing when the part is gone, holds no unit, or the player does not command the ship. Every player sees the result message in the center of the screen and in the chat.
1114. A content error in `HotCargoActions` shows when the rules load, in the log and the error overlay: two actions with one ID, an action without an ID, an action without `Contents`, `MessageKey` and `Handler`, a `Handler` that no area added (requirement 1146), or a negative weight.
1115. Sealed Case, action "Open the case". No dialog and no scan first. The case becomes an Opened Case (`yarden_zamir.opened_case`) in its slot, plus one of: Corporate Access Codes (`yarden_zamir.access_codes`, weight 60) or a Tracer Beacon (`yarden_zamir.tracer_beacon`, weight 40 + 0.3 × heat). A station's comms buy the codes for 0.5 money units each ("Sell corporate access codes.", `codes_fence`). Stations do not trade these items.
1116. A Tracer Beacon in a player ship (stored or carried by crew) adds `Events.TracerHeatPerMinute` (default 3) heat once a minute per beacon, with a chat line. Loose beacons do not count. Its action "Space the beacon" destroys it. A hit on its storage always destroys it.
1117. A hand-over item has forms: the item, and the `Becomes` item of each of its actions. The hand-over counts every form: as held, for the lost check (requirement 1103), for its comms line, and for the unit that a job takes back when it leaves the log. The Courier contact takes a sealed or an opened case.
1118. An opened case at the Courier hand-over: the contact reacts. Weight 40: it does not notice, full pay. Weight 30: it notices, and the job's money reward drops to 80 %. Weight 15 + 0.15 × heat: it keeps the case, gets its own hostile allegiance and hunts the players within 4,000 of its spot; the job fails. Weight 15 + 0.1 × heat: it jumps out of the system with the case; the job fails. The page and the chat show the result. Outcome fields: `HandOverPay`, `ContactTurns` (`Hostile`, `Leaves`), `Chat`.
1119. A mission offer's accept button in the comms missions tab updates every frame: room for the mission's item (requirement 1101) and the contract fee (requirement 102). A player who makes room sees the button react again without a new selection.
1120. Extraction (`ChainExtraction`): the transport's storage holds real processors, 1 or 2 in each free storage tile. The pickup page has no strip choice. To take the cargo, wreck the transport after the pickup: its storage drops the processors as loose crates, with vanilla drop rates. Courier Run (`ChainCourierHit`) uses the same transport.
1121. Prototype Retrieval broker (`prototype_broker`): "Ask for more" is a clear deal: 0.5 money units more and heat +10. There is no roll.
1122. Patrol listen. A built vanilla sensor array (`cosmoteer.sensor_array`) that is not destroyed, on a ship that the player commands, can listen to a faction ship (the `FactionShip` hail target of requirement 769: not a station, not hostile). When a player selects the sensor array, the parts card shows the button "Listen to patrol chatter". The button is greyed out, with the reason in its tooltip, while another listen runs, or no faction ship that was not heard yet is within `Events.ListenRange` (1,500, hull to hull). A press sends the MP input `HotCargoPatrolListen` (the sensor array part). The sim picks the nearest such faction ship. One listen runs at a time for the fleet. Limit: the sensor array listens without power and crew; revisit when listening should cost crew.
1123. A listen lasts `Events.ListenSeconds` (8) game seconds. A progress bar above the sensor array shows to every player. The sim tick stops the listen, with a center message and a chat line, when the sensor array is destroyed or gone, the faction ship is gone or hostile, the distance is over `ListenRange`, or there is combat: the listening ship, or an enemy of it within `ListenCombatRadius` (800), fired in the last `ListenCombatSeconds` (5). A stopped listen changes nothing, and the ship can be heard later. At the end the sim random generator rolls the success chance: a line from `ListenChanceAtNoHeat` (100 %) at heat 0 to `ListenChanceAtMaxHeat` (60 %) at heat 100. Success: heat `ListenSuccessHeat` (−4). Failure: heat `ListenFailHeat` (+5), they log your question. Both scale with `Events.HeatScale`. A center message and a chat line name the outcome and the heat change. The faction ship counts as heard (once per ship). Patrol Chatter (EVENTS.md 34) keeps only the clear deal. Tests: autotest `events-listen`, co-op step `events.patrol-listen`, unit tests `PatrolListenModelTests`.
1124. Hulk loot. When a vanilla abandoned ship (label `abandoned`, not the relic) first shows up live, the sim tick rolls once (sim random generator): with chance `Events.HulkLootChance` (0.6) it gets a hulk kind from `mod/events/hulks.rules` (by `Weight`, kinds at `MaxPerRun` left out) and one of the kind's `Loot` outcomes (weights `Weight` + `WeightPerHeat` × heat). The vanilla cargo in its cargo tiles goes. The outcome's `Cargo` (`Amount` + `PerTier` × (tier − 1)) goes into cargo tiles that take it, then where the game puts it (tanks such as a hyperdrive). Its stasis spawner remembers the cargo. A hulk with room for none of the cargo gets no loot and no messages. Without loot the hulk keeps its vanilla cargo and stays quiet. Kinds: Abandoned Ship and Cold Hulk (EVENTS.md 30, 31). A content error in the file shows in the log and the error overlay, and hulks then get no loot.
1125. Within `AbandonedTriggerDistance` (800) of a hulk with loot, a center message and a chat line show the kind's `Text`, once per hulk. The players take the loot the vanilla way: they wreck or salvage the hulk and tractor in what drops (vanilla drop rates). The hold is the parts that got loot (the cargo storage parts for an empty outcome). When a part of the hold is destroyed, or the hulk leaves the sim and is not in stasis, a center message and a chat line show the outcome's `Text`, once. The corporate trap is a Tracer Beacon among bait steel: shots destroy it (requirement 1116), careful salvage drops it, and the players can space it.
1126. A loot outcome with `Blast` (Cold Hulk, "the reactor coughs": 0.2) blows when the hold breaks open: each player ship whose hull is within `Events.HulkBlastRadius` (300) of the hulk loses `Blast` × max health on its 6 parts nearest to the hulk (vaults left out, parts keep 1 health). No roll: the order is distance, then location. The outcome message adds the hull damage line. Tests: autotest `events-abandoned`, co-op step `events.hulk-loot`.
1146. Item action handlers. An action in `HotCargoActions` can name a C# `Handler` (`ItemActions.Handlers`, added by an area in Register). The rules check at load that the handler exists (requirement 1114). The handler runs first, in the item action MP input (requirement 1113): it gives the message and says whether the action used the unit. When it did not, the unit stays and the message still shows. The action button's tooltip adds the handler's live lines while it shows.
1147. Rescue Beacon (`yarden_zamir.rescue_beacon`). Trade stations stock it and sell it in their vanilla trade window, like other goods: a `StationResourceTradeDeltas` entry gives a station about 1 or 2 (1 % to 1.5 % of its untyped storage tiles). Its price is `Events.RescueBeaconPrice` (0.3) money units at the current tier, times the station's heat markup (requirement 655) as for every good; a station buys it back at half that price. Its action "Fire the beacon" (handler `rescue`) starts a Rescue Tow mission (`RescueMission` in spawns.rules): the tow objective of requirement 88 with the firing ship as the towed ship, `MinStationDistance` 0 and `FriendlyOnly` (stations whose owner is not hostile to the ship). No such station, or a tug already comes for the ship: the action does nothing, and the beacon stays. Else the firing player pays `Events.RescueFee` (0.4) money units at the current tier, or all their money when that is less, so a rescue never fails for money. Heat rises by `Events.RescueHeat` (10) × `Events.HeatScale`. The tooltip shows both numbers.
1148. The tug (`RescueTug` in spawns.rules: a civilian trade ship without AI) spawns 600 to 800 from the ship and flies to it. Within `Events.RescueTowLength` (150, hull to hull) it takes the ship on the tow line and flies to the station: past the line's length, the sim tick gives the ship the tug's velocity plus a pull of `RescueTowPull` × the extra length per game second. The tug's course goes around the heat zones of stars, `RescueSunMargin` (300) outside their edge (`RescueModel.Waypoint`), unless the zone covers the station. New orders follow every 2 game seconds. When the tow objective completes (the ship within 1,200 of the station), a message says so, and the tug flies off and leaves the sim after `RescueTugLeaveSeconds` (20). When the tug is destroyed, the tow stops with a message and the mission stays. Everything runs in the sim tick from sim state, with spawn seeds from the sim random generator, so co-op stays in step. Tests: autotest `events-rescue`, co-op step `events.rescue`, unit tests `RescueModelTests`.
