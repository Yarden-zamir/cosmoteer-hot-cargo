## 46. World actions

Design rule (section 2): risky actions and gambles happen in the world. A dialog offers only clear deals. This section covers item actions in storage, the Sealed Case and the gambles that moved out of dialogs.

1111. An item's resource rules list its context actions in `HotCargoActions`: `ID`, `NameKey`, `ToolTipKey`, `Becomes`, `Contents`, `MessageKey`. When a player selects a storage part that holds a unit of such an item, on a ship that the player commands, the parts card (lower left) shows one button per action. The buttons sit with the vanilla part trigger and toggle buttons. A button shows the item's icon; its tooltip shows the action name and text.
1112. An action takes one unit out of the selected part. `Becomes` puts its item into the freed slot. One of `Contents` (weights `Weight` + `WeightPerHeat` × heat) goes into the same part: the freed slot first, else a slot that takes it, else it drifts loose beside the ship. An action without `Becomes` and `Contents` uses the unit up. The roll uses the sim random generator.
1113. A press sends the MP input `HotCargoItemAction` (part, item, action), so it runs on every machine in co-op. It does nothing when the part is gone, holds no unit, or the player does not command the ship. Every player sees the result message in the center of the screen and in the chat.
1114. A content error in `HotCargoActions` shows when the rules load, in the log and the error overlay: two actions with one ID, an action without an ID, an action without `Contents` and without `MessageKey`, or a negative weight.
1115. Sealed Case, action "Open the case". No dialog and no scan first. The case becomes an Opened Case (`yarden_zamir.opened_case`) in its slot, plus one of: Corporate Access Codes (`yarden_zamir.access_codes`, weight 60) or a Tracer Beacon (`yarden_zamir.tracer_beacon`, weight 40 + 0.3 × heat). A station's comms buy the codes for 0.5 money units each ("Sell corporate access codes.", `codes_fence`). Stations do not trade these items.
1116. A Tracer Beacon in a player ship (stored or carried by crew) adds `Events.TracerHeatPerMinute` (default 3) heat once a minute per beacon, with a chat line. Loose beacons do not count. Its action "Space the beacon" destroys it. A hit on its storage always destroys it.
1117. A hand-over item has forms: the item, and the `Becomes` item of each of its actions. The hand-over counts every form: as held, for the lost check (requirement 1103), for its comms line, and for the unit that a job takes back when it leaves the log. The Courier contact takes a sealed or an opened case.
1118. An opened case at the Courier hand-over: the contact reacts. Weight 40: it does not notice, full pay. Weight 30: it notices, and the job's money reward drops to 80 %. Weight 15 + 0.15 × heat: it keeps the case, gets its own hostile allegiance and hunts the players within 4,000 of its spot; the job fails. Weight 15 + 0.1 × heat: it jumps out of the system with the case; the job fails. The page and the chat show the result. Outcome fields: `HandOverPay`, `ContactTurns` (`Hostile`, `Leaves`), `Chat`.
1119. A mission offer's accept button in the comms missions tab updates every frame: room for the mission's item (requirement 1101) and the contract fee (requirement 102). A player who makes room sees the button react again without a new selection.
1120. Extraction (`ChainExtraction`): the transport's storage holds real processors, 1 or 2 in each free storage tile. The pickup page has no strip choice. To take the cargo, wreck the transport after the pickup: its storage drops the processors as loose crates, with vanilla drop rates. Courier Run (`ChainCourierHit`) uses the same transport.
1121. Prototype Retrieval broker (`prototype_broker`): "Ask for more" is a clear deal: 0.5 money units more and heat +10. There is no roll.
