## 43. Relay lockdowns and the timed stash

Two rare risks with a price tag, like the FTL beacons and the stores of a sector. Code: `src/HotCargo/Relays`, `src/HotCargo/Stash`. Rules and strings: `mod/relays`, `mod/stash`. Numbers: the `Relays` and `Stash` tunables sections. Pure math: `Relays/RelaysModel.cs`, `Stash/StashModel.cs`.

### Relay lockdowns

950. **Lockdown.** In a system with a lockdown, the relays refuse relay jumps until the lockdown opens. A blind jump (requirement 650) always passes. `Relays.Enabled` turns lockdowns off.
951. **Rare and paced.** When the players are in a system for the first time in a visit, each neighbour system that they never visited rolls once per run, in ID order, with chance `ChancePerSystem` (0.15). The roll is seeded by the galaxy seed and the node. At most one system is marked at a time, and the Buyer's sector is never marked. A neighbour rolls only while the pacer allows a big event (`Pacing.Current`, interface `IBigEventPacer`). Until the pursuit director (spec 42) is on main, the local pacer allows a lockdown from sector index `MinSector` (2), at most `MaxPerRun` (2) per run, and `MinGapSectors` (2) after the sector of the last one. A lockdown counts when the players enter it. Limit: the local pacer knows only lockdowns; switch `Pacing.Current` to the director's pacer when it lands.
952. **Kinds.** The mark picks a kind by weight: `ClosedWeight`, `FeeWeight` and `PiratesWeight` (1 each). A system without a controlling faction can only get pirates.
953. **On the map before the jump.** The marked system shows a tag under its name on the galaxy map, like the HOT ZONE tag (requirement 645): RELAY CLOSED, RELAY TOLL or PIRATE RELAY. Its tooltip says what the lockdown asks and the ways out. A chat line names the marked system when the mark is made. While the lockdown of the current system blocks, the current system shows the same tag and line. A mark on a system that is no longer a neighbour goes away.
954. **Arrival.** Entering the marked system starts its lockdown: a toast ("RELAY LOCKDOWN") and a chat line with the ways out. A ready click (`MPReadyFtlJumpInput`) does nothing while the relays block; the spin-up window greys out ENGAGE and shows a red line with the reason. Leaving the system ends the lockdown.
955. **Closed.** The system's faction closes the relays. They open after `ClosedSeconds` (420) game seconds of the visit; 0 keeps them closed. Ways out: a blind jump, or wait (heat rises meanwhile).
956. **Toll.** The system's faction asks a toll: `FeeUnits` (0.6) + `FeePerHeat` (0.006) × heat event money units (requirement 73). Every station of the controlling faction answers calls and shows the toll page in comms; "Pay the toll" is a shared choice (domain `relays`). The chooser pays, and the relays open. A station that collects a toll never refuses to talk (requirement 657). Ways out: pay, or a blind jump.
957. **Pirates.** On the first tick of the visit, pirates spawn next to the relay nearest to the fleet (`mod/relays/guards.rules`, tag `hc_relay_guard`): a hostile group of 2 to 3 combat ships of the local factions at the system tier. The group count goes through the co-op scaling (requirement 332, `Scaling.EnemyScale.Count`). The relays open when no guard is left: destroyed, gone, or under a truce with the players. A guard in stasis (far from the fleet) still holds the relay.
958. **Bribe.** Each guard answers calls with a multiselect page (requirement 590): credits (slider up to twice the price) and up to `BribeResourceKinds` (3) resource kinds that the human fleet holds (sliders up to the amount held; most value first; never tech). Price = `BribeUnits` (1.2) event money units. A resource counts at `BribeResourceRate` (0.8) × its station buy price. The page shows the offer and the chance live: 0 up to `BribeMinShare` (0.5) of the price, then a straight line to 1 at the full price. Submit rolls the chance; on success the chooser pays the credits, the fleet hands over the resources (the chooser's ships first), every guard gets a truce with the players, and the relays open. A guard in stasis gets its truce when it comes out. Relays registers its comms provider before Diplomacy, and the page replaces the vanilla pirate surrender dialogue, so a guard's page has only relay options.
959. **No deal.** A failed bribe or "Come and take it." ends the talks for the visit. Ways out then: fight the guards, or a blind jump.
960. **Determinism.** The marks, the lockdown, the guards and the bribe rolls change only in the sim tick, in the system switch and in MP inputs (shared choice and multiselect inputs). The bribe roll is seeded by the guard, the chooser and the sim tick. The run state saves the marks, the rolled nodes, the counts and the active lockdown with its guards. The map tags, the tooltip and the spin-up line are UI only.
961. **Tests.** Headless: `relays-map` (a real roll marks a neighbour; map tag and tooltip; the jump in starts the lockdown), `relays-closed` (ENGAGE blocked, the closed time, a blind jump out), `relays-fee` (the toll page and payment), `relays-pirates` (bribe with credits, bribe with cargo, fight, a failed bribe and the blind jump). Co-op: step `relays.bribe` in the session `coop-combat` (the client's bribe draft, the host's submit, the relays open on both machines). Unit tests: `RelaysModelTests`.

### Timed stash

970. **Offer.** A system offers a "Rumoured Stash" with chance `Stash.OfferChance` (0.35), set on the spawner group before the system generates. It sits in the bounty list of the nearest trade station, with no faction, like a shady offer (requirement 38). The stash is a place in open space (an undiscovered target area). Contract terms: fee 0.2 standard mission pay, flat heat 2, no informant.
971. **Found first.** The chance that the corporation found the stash first = `FoundFirstBase` (0.1) + `FoundFirstPerMinute` (0.04) × game minutes since the offer spawned, at most `FoundFirstMax` (0.9). So 34 % after 6 minutes.
972. **Arrival.** A human ship within 1,200 of the stash rolls with the chance at that moment. The roll removes the site marker; until then the stash objective owns it (requirement 996). Not found first: loot. Found first: empty with share `EmptyShare` (0.5), else an ambush: the shady trap ambush (requirement 40, hunter AI, sector tier + 1, a scaled rules group). The ambush mission ends without loot when the ambushers are gone, or when the players leave the system.
973. **Loot.** `LootMoneyFactor` (3) × a standard mission's money at the stash's tier, and a standard mission's fame. With chance `LootTechChance` (0.5), also `LootTechUnits` (1) tech in the vaults; a toast says when the vaults have no room.
974. **Shown risk.** The objective line in the offer and in the mission log reads "Find the stash. Found first: 35%. Tech: 50%." The line follows the chance while the window shows it: a new percent rebuilds the objective lines.
975. **Save and determinism.** The objective saves the site, the offer time, the roll seed, the outcome and the ambushers. A save with an accepted stash does not load without the mod. The roll uses a seed from the offer and the run's game time, from the sim tick.
976. **Tests.** Headless: `stash-rolls` (the offer chance, the found-first line and its growth, forced loot without and with tech, empty, ambush, a real roll). Unit tests: `StashModelTests`.
