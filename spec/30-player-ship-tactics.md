## 30. Player ship tactics

Dragon Age style tactics for the players' own ships. Code: `src/HotCargo/ShipAi`. Pure model: `ShipAiModel.cs`. Strings: `mod/shipai/strings.en.rules`. Tunables: the `ShipAi` section.

400. Each player ship can have a tactics list: up to 16 rows of "conditions → action". A row has 1 to 8 condition terms (requirement 416). A ship without rows has no tactics state.
401. **Conditions**: always, my health below X % (health as in requirement 47), my shields below X % (shield charge over shield capacity; a ship without shields never matches), I lost a part of a kind (weapon, thruster, shield, Tech Vault; a destroyed part that is not rebuilt yet), an enemy within R / no enemy within R (edge to edge, enemies on the team radar), an enemy targets me, an ally under attack (an enemy has an attack order on another human ship), my ammo below X % (the supplies that parts burn, as in UX.md V7, without battery, hyperium and tech), my battery below X %, an enemy of a class on radar (smaller or larger by blueprint value, or a station: a ship without thrusters), at least N enemies on radar, I carry Stolen Tech, my Tech Vault is damaged. The Hot Cargo conditions are in requirement 413.
402. **Actions** have three kinds. Target: nearest, weakest (lowest health), strongest (highest blueprint value), the enemy that attacks an ally, the enemy that attacks a ship with a Tech Vault. Move: keep at range (the target at `KiteRangeFraction` of the longest weapon range, edge to edge), close in (to `CloseInDistance`), hold position, retreat to the nearest ally, retreat and repair (move away from the nearest enemy until `RetreatDistance` away; the crew repair as usual), flee and jump (a local FTL jump to the friendly ship furthest from the nearest enemy when the vanilla jump rules allow it, else flee on thrusters), follow my first ship, guard (my first ship, the vault ship or the weakest ally: follow it and attack the enemy that attacks it), collect tech crates (requirement 414). Facing: requirement 415. Fire, for a weapon group (all weapons or part control group 1 to 10): at will, focus (the part that the vanilla AI values most), at their weapons, at their engines (the nearest such part), point defense only, hold fire, disruptors (requirement 414). Ties go to the nearest ship, then to the sim order.
403. With `Auto` on, the ship evaluates its rows every `TickSeconds` (default 1 s) of game time. Rows run from top to bottom. A row matches when its conditions hold (requirement 416) and its action can run now (a target action needs that target, keep at range and close in need an enemy; retreat to an ally, follow and guard need that ship). The first matching target row picks the target, the first matching move row picks the movement, and the first matching facing row picks the facing. With a target and no move row, the ship attacks it with the vanilla attack order.
404. Each weapon follows the first matching Fire row of its group or of all weapons (a disruptors row only for disruptor weapons, requirement 414), else fires at will. Hold fire and point defense only take away the fire permission (point defense weapons keep it under point defense only). Focus, weapons and engines give the weapon a part target on the tactics target (disruptors: requirement 414); point defense keeps its own targets. A part target that the player set stays.
405. The tactics text form is `HC1:` and rows joined by `;`, each `terms>Act[=n]` (terms: requirement 417). Copy and paste use it. Bad text (an unknown name, a missing or out-of-range number, more than 16 rows or more than 8 terms in a row) is refused with a message and changes nothing.
406. Presets are starting rows that the player can change: Aggressive, Defensive, Sniper, Escort, Vault Guard (`ShipAiModel.PresetRows`). The player's own presets follow them in the list (requirement 422).
407. The rows and the Auto switch are saved with the ship design: `Ship.SaveDesign` writes the nodes `HotCargoTactics` and `HotCargoTacticsAuto`, and `Ship.Load` reads them. The game reads design nodes by name, so a design with tactics loads without the mod (without tactics). The build menu's "Load ship (replace)" copies the tactics of the design to the live ship through the tactics input; a design without tactics keeps the ship's tactics. The live ship's full tactics state is saved in the career save, as the path `HotCargoTactics` of the ship. A co-op join and a resync carry it the same way. A design or a save with bad tactics text loads without tactics and shows the error.
408. Player orders come first. While the ship has a player order (a command with a player ID) that is not done, the tactics wait and drop their own order and part targets. A move order is done when the ship arrives. The tactics remove only the order that they gave. Auto off, `ShipAi.Enabled` off, or a ship that is no longer a human ship drops the decision the same way. The rows stay.
409. Every edit (the editor, a paste, a preset, a design load, apply to selected) sends the whole row list and the Auto switch as the MP input `HotCargoShipTactics`. Only a player who can command the ship can change it. The editor shows the sim's rows, so an edit by another player shows in the open window.
410. **Editor**: Ctrl+K, or the "AI" button in the ships card (one selected own ship), opens a window: the Auto switch, the preset list with Apply and Delete, Copy, Paste, Clear, the preset name with Save as preset, Apply to selected, one block per row (requirement 419) and a status line (off, waiting for a player order, the current target). The number of each row in effect now is green. The window scrolls. The window uses the vanilla window, drop list and button styles. Autotests never use the system clipboard.
411. Determinism: the tactics run in the sim tick (`EventHub.Tick`), walk the ships in sim order, and read only sim state. They give vanilla commands with sim command IDs. The tactics state is part of the ship's integrity hash. They also run after a win, because the career goes on.
412. Limit: the tactics control only player ships; they are not a vanilla `ShipAI`. A vanilla AI on a player ship would change comms, stasis and ship splits. Revisit if the game adds an autopilot for player ships.

### Hot Cargo hooks, condition chains, fleet tools and facing

413. **Hot Cargo conditions** read other areas through their public state: heat stage at least N (`RunState.StagesStarted`; N is 1 to the number of heat stages, `Heat.Stages`: the editor offers these, and the tactics input refuses a higher N; stored text up to `ShipAiModel.MaxStage` still loads, and such a term never holds), hunters in the system (`HunterSpawner.Hunters`), Strike wave within S seconds (`Run.SecondsToStrikeWave`: only at full heat in the last stage), the tech loss timer runs (`LossTimer.IsRunning`), tech crates within R (tech nuggets that the team sees, edge distance from the hull), an enemy holds our tech (an enemy on radar with an entry in `TechState.HunterHeld`), an enemy calls for help (an enemy on radar whose `CallStrategy.Calling` is true).
414. **Hot Cargo actions**. Target: the enemy that holds our tech, the enemy that calls for help (nearest first). Move: collect tech crates: the ship flies to the nearest tech crate on radar until its hull is `CollectDistance` away, and gives the vanilla crew collect order (`ResourceManager.ManualResourceTransfer`, as `SalvageJob.OnPartSalvaged` does) for each tech crate within the crew's safe distance (`Crew.LoseHomeShipWarningDistance`, the limit of the vanilla collect tool). It can run while a crate is on radar and the ship has room for tech. Fire: disruptors: only disruptor weapons follow this row; other weapons skip it and follow the next matching Fire row. A disruptor weapon is a weapon of a `DisruptorParts` part or of a part that stores `DisruptorAmmo` now (a missile launcher set to EMP missiles). It aims at the part that the vanilla AI values most on the nearest enemy that calls for help, else on the tactics target.
415. **Facing**: an action of its own kind with a side: nose, left side, right side, stern. The first matching facing row turns the tactics attack order with the vanilla rotation options: `RotationTarget` = the target, `RotationMode` = follow angle relative, `Rotation` = 0°, +90°, −90° or 180° from the nose (`BaseFollowCommand.GetWorldRotation`), less the design's `Ship.FlightDirection`, because the nose points at the ship rotation plus that direction. It can run while an enemy is on radar. Without a facing row, the attack order uses the vanilla best attack rotation. A change of the facing row gives a new attack order.
416. **Condition chains**: each term has its condition, its parameter, a NOT switch and a connector to the term before it, AND or OR. AND binds tighter than OR: "A AND B OR C AND D" means (A and B) or (C and D). This covers every combination without parentheses. Every term is evaluated on every tick, so each one keeps its memory (requirement 418).
417. **Chain text**: the terms of a row are `[!]Cond[=n]` joined by `&` (AND) or `|` (OR), for example `HC1:!CarryingTech&HealthBelow=40|HeatStageAtLeast=3>CollectTech`. A row with one term without NOT is the old form, so all old `HC1:` text reads and writes unchanged. There is no migration code.
418. **Anti-flicker**: a threshold term (percent, distance or seconds) that holds keeps holding until its value passes the threshold by `HysteresisMargin` (percent terms: points × 100; distance and seconds terms: a fraction of the threshold). A threshold term keeps a new value at least `MinHoldSeconds` of game time. NOT applies after both. Other terms follow their fact at once. The term memory (value and game second of each term) is sim state: it is saved with the ship, is part of its integrity hash, and resets when the rows change or the decision drops (requirement 408).
419. **Editor rows**: a row block has one line per term: IF (first term) or an AND/OR switch, a NOT switch, the condition, its parameter, and a remove button while the row has more than one term. The first line also holds the action, its parameter, "+ cond" (up to 8 terms), up, down and remove.
420. Every term edit (add, remove, NOT, AND/OR, condition, parameter) sends the whole row list as one tactics input (requirement 409).
421. **Apply to selected**: the button sends this window's rows and Auto switch to every other selected ship that the local player commands, one tactics input per ship. The input checks the permission for each ship (requirement 409). The button shows the number of those ships and is off with none.
422. **Personal presets**: Save as preset stores the current rows under the typed name (at most 32 characters, no line breaks) in the local file `hot_cargo_tactics_presets.txt` next to the game settings (`Paths.RootSavePath`), one "name TAB text" line each. A preset with the same name is replaced. The list shows them after the built-ins as "Mine: name"; Delete removes the selected one. The file is a player preference, not sim state: each player has their own. A bad line is skipped. Applying a personal preset is an edit like any other (requirement 409).
