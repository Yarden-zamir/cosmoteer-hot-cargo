# Pay it forward (spec 1369 to 1371): outclassed players get help from the Wayfarers, who have their own allegiance (friends of the
# players, enemies of their enemies). After the fight they hail with the "pay it forward" page, repair and leave.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set StationEventChance 0
ev_set ArrivalEventChance 0
ev_set CourierChance 0
ev_set CooldownSeconds 0
godmode on
# A sure roll and a low bar, so one enemy counts as outclassing.
fr_set PayForwardChance 1
fr_set PayForwardOutclassRatio 0.01
expect fr_wayfarers == 0
dip_spawn enemy 900
pause off
waitfor fr_wayfarers >= 1 20
expect fr_wayfarer_sides == 1
# The fight ends: after the quiet time their leader hails with the page, and the fleet repairs itself.
fr_damage_wayfarer
expect fr_wayfarer_health < 0.6
fr_kill_enemies
waitfor ev_pending == 1 40
expect fr_wayfarer_health > 0.99
expectevent pay_it_forward
expectcomms carrier Pay it forward 1
ev_comms carrier We will
expect ev_pending == 0
waitfor fr_wayfarers == 0 10
expect fr_forward_done == 1
# At most once per run: a new qualifying fight (the system may roll again here) brings no second fleet, also after a save and load.
save forward
load forward
wait 8
expect fr_forward_done == 1
fr_help_reset
dip_spawn enemy 900
pause off
wait 10
expect fr_wayfarers == 0
quit
