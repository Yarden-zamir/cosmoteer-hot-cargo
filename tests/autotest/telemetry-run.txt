# Telemetry: a longer scripted run that makes data for a first report (tools/telemetry). It visits five systems,
# takes missions and contracts, raises heat through the stages, fights hunters, sells tech, reloads and ends with a win.
# The test commands stand in for the player: heat and fame steps for missions, damagehunters for won fights.
# The checks are on the telemetry; the game areas have their own tests.
newgame run
wait 8
godmode
tacticlog on
# The starter ship cannot outlast every stage of five systems (crew die in fires, which godmode does not stop).
# Fewer live hunters and frequent won fights keep it alive; a balance analysis must leave both out.
maxhunters 2
# System 1 (tier 1): the Start event, a contract, an escort, a sale, then time heat and a Warning scout.
choose 1
wait 2
hc_generate all
wait 3
contract_money 200000
contract_pick any
contract_accept
tostation
wait 3
tech sell 1
wait 30
hc_accept escort
hc_escort_goto
pause off
wait 5
hc_escort_arrive
wait 3
damagehostiles 0.05
fame 15
heat 10
wait 30
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 30
log system1_warning
wait 20
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 25
log system1
# System 2: a mission's worth of fame, Patrol, a vault hit and an event.
jump
pause off
wait 15
fame 15
heat 25
wait 20
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 25
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 25
vault damage 0.5
wait 3
event distress_beacon
choose 1
wait 5
log system2
# System 3: Hunt, a save and a load in the middle of the visit.
jump
pause off
wait 15
heat 45
wait 20
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 20
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 20
save telerun
wait 3
load telerun
pause off
wait 20
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 20
log system3
# System 4 (tier 7): a starter ship does not outlast a tier 7 Hunt, so it sells at once and leaves at Patrol.
jump
pause off
wait 5
damagehostiles 0.05
tostation
wait 3
tech sell 1
wait 25
heat 25
wait 15
damagehostiles 0.05
wait 15
log system4
# System 5: the Buyer and the win.
jump
pause off
wait 20
buyer spawn
wait 3
buyer goto
wait 15
buyer goto
wait 3
buyer sell 2
wait 45
expect won == 1
log won
wait 15
teleflush
telecount system.enter >= 4
telecount heat.stage >= 3
telecount combat.spawn >= 3
telecount econ.trade >= 2
telecount mission.accepted >= 2
telecount run.won == 1
telestats
expect tele_dropped == 0
expect tele_overhead_pct < 1
quit
