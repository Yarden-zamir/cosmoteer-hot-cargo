# Spec 1126: a job that ends while the players are in another system loses its markers on the next arrival in its system.
# The Black Box's first chain stage owns target area markers (the guarded wreck). The players accept it, leave, abandon the job
# in the other system, and come back.
newgame run
wait 8
ev_set Enabled false
chain_force black_box
chain_tostation
chain_accept
expect chain_accepted == 1
hc_markers_mark HotCargoChain
expect hc_markers_left >= 1
jump
wait 8
chain_abandon
expect chain_accepted == 0
jump back
wait 8
expect hc_markers_left == 0
quit
