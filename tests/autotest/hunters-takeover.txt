# Hunters never join the players (playtest 2026-11-13, spec 33, 1002, 1003). The players take over a hunter ship, then new
# hunters arrive: none of them has a human index, and all are enemies of the players. A wave that arrives while the other hunters
# wait in stasis joins their side.
newgame run
wait 8

wait 1

expect hunterstaken == 1

expect huntershostile == 1
