# Co-op factions prices and the blind switch, host side (spec 644, 650, 655). Prices are sim state: the client's trades at a fence and at a heat markup
# pass the sim's exact price check (MPMoveResourcesInput) on both machines, with no desync.
mphost {PORT}
mpstart 2
mp pu_set Enabled false
mp ev_set Enabled false
mp godmode on
mp tostation
waitfor mpmark >= 1 180
mp fa_roles - station
mp fa_base
mp mpmark 2
waitfor mpmark >= 3 180
expect fa_rivalsell == 1.5
mp heat 80
mp mpmark 4
waitfor mpmark >= 5 180
expect fa_markup == 1.2
expect desyncs == 0
waitfor mpconnected == 0 240
quit
