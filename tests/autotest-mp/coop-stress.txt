# Co-op session: performance measure (spec 870 to 873). A heavy fight: the Strike stage with repeat waves, personalities, teams and
# cover among rocks, 60 wrecks, events and diplomacy hails on. selfprof and frameprof run on both machines (SELFPROF and FRAMEPROF lines).
# See coop-core.txt for "on 1" and steps.
mphost {PORT}
mpstart 2
mp godmode on
mp maxhunters 16
mp strikeinterval 20
mp airocks 10 900
mp wrecks 60
mp aispawn pirates 4
mp aispawn monolith 4
mp aispawn imperium 3
mp heat 100

step telemetry.stress
selfprof on
on 1 selfprof on
wait 20
selfprof reset
on 1 selfprof reset
frameprof on
on 1 frameprof on
wait 90
selfprof report coop
frameprof report coop
on 1 selfprof report client
on 1 frameprof report client
telestats
expect desyncs == 0
expect selfprof_pct_tick < 25

end
